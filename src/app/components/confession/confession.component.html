UNDERSTANDING SIN

Mortal and venial: what’s the difference?

Mortal sin

Breaks our friendship with God. All three conditions must be present together:

  1. Grave matter: a seriously wrong act.
  2. Full knowledge: knowing it is seriously wrong.
  3. Deliberate consent: freely choosing the act.

Venial sin

Wounds our relationship with God without breaking it.

Involves less serious matter, or grave matter when full knowledge or complete consent is missing.

Seriousness depends on the act and its circumstances. Selecting a prompt does not determine whether your sin was mortal. If you’re unsure, bring it to a priest without feeling you must classify it first.

Both call for repentance. Mortal sins must be confessed; confessing venial sins is encouraged.
